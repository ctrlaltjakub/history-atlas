AN INDEPENDENT PODCAST CATALOGUE

About the project

A way to browse The Rest Is History by historical period and place. The podcast is hosted by Tom Holland and Dominic Sandbrook and produced by Goalhanger. This is an unofficial project.

Series first

Episodes released together as a series appear as one coloured tile throughout the timeline, map, index and topic lists. Open a series to see its episodes in release order, then choose a part for its description, listening links and individual placement. Series memberships are based on the official archive and checked against the public feed. Standalone episodes appear individually.

The timeline

Each series is placed at the earliest historical date covered by its episodes. Episodes without a series use their own date. Tiles sharing a date stack from the top. The busiest date sets the number of rows for the whole table, so scrolling never changes its height. No rows are reserved across gaps within a series. Dates are approximate; an episode’s details explain its placement.

Gaps between dates are compressed, so distance is not a fixed number of years. Search, era and region filters match individual episodes, then show their series once. In a filtered timeline, the earliest matching dated part determines the series’ position. Opening the series always shows all its parts.

The map

The map uses the Equal Earth projection and approximate subject locations. A series appears once, at its first mappable episode in release order; its parts may cover other places. From an episode’s details, Show on the map highlights that episode’s own location. Zoom to separate clusters, or select a shared location to browse its series and standalone episodes.

Dates and locations are independent: an episode may appear on the map without a timeline date, or on the timeline without a map location. Undated parts also appear under Episodes not on a timeline, grouped by series and topic. The Worldwide group contains items without a single geographic setting. Modern coastlines and borders are for orientation.

The catalogue & listening links

Pocket Casts opens the matching episode. Spotify and Apple Podcasts open a verified episode where available; search fallbacks are labelled. YouTube links are searches. Original public audio is playable here. Location shortcuts reveal an episode in the full catalogue and clear filters that could hide it.
