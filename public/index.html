A NOTE FROM THE MAP ROOM

A guide, with room for nuance.

This is an independent, unofficial companion to The Rest Is History, hosted by Tom Holland and Dominic Sandbrook and produced by Goalhanger.

How the timeline works

Episodes run from earlier to later by the beginning of the historical period they cover. Each region has its own lane. Dates and ranges are editorial approximations, not timestamps or claims that a whole episode concerns a single year. Open an episode for its period and placement note.

Space expands where there are more stories, so the distance between cards does not represent a fixed number of years. The era buttons select stories by the beginning of their period. Open broad surveys to see their full date range. The atlas opens around the first civilisations; scroll left to reach prehistory.

An episode appears in the region most central to its story. International subjects have a shared world lane. Myths, questions and discussions without a meaningful historical date live in “Beyond the timeline”.

The collection & listening links

Pocket Casts buttons open the matching episode. Spotify opens the matching episode when verified, or an explicitly labelled search. Apple Podcasts opens the episode when an exact match is available; otherwise its button says “Find episode”. YouTube links are explicitly labelled searches. The original public audio is also playable here.
